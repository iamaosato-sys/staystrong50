Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

StayStrong50 is a free strength check and training app for adults aged 50 and over provided by Entwisle Fitness, a personal training business based on the Fylde Coast, Lancashire, UK. For any privacy question or request, contact us through entwislefitness.co.uk.

Your workout data stays on your device

Everything you enter — your levels, sessions and badges — is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. We do not have servers that receive it, and we cannot see it. There is no account and no sign-up.

This includes your age, which reference ranges you chose (women's, men's or both), your strength check results and your daily "how do I feel" answers. They are used only inside the app on your device to show comparisons and adjust your sessions.

The health check you complete at the start is not saved or sent anywhere.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people use the app and which features are used (for example "workout started"). This uses cookies and sends technical information such as your approximate location, device and browser type, and pages viewed to Google. IP addresses are anonymised. We never send your age, test results, workout numbers or health answers to Google.

If you click Reject, or ignore the banner, Google Analytics is not loaded. You can change your choice any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the app.

Lawful basis: your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Google may process data outside the UK under its standard contractual clauses. See Google's privacy policy.

Hosting

The app is hosted by Vercel Inc. Like any web host, Vercel processes basic technical data (such as IP address) to deliver the site securely. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, and to withdraw consent. Because workout data is only on your device, you control it directly:

  • Export: Settings → Export backup (JSON) or history (CSV).
  • Delete: Settings → Delete all my data, or clear your browser's site data.
  • Withdraw analytics consent: Cookie settings → Reject.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Children

StayStrong50 is intended for adults aged 50 and over. It is not intended for children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top.
